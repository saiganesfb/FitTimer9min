Chain Links ("Don't Break the Chain")

Atomic Habits: "Never miss twice" — Seinfeld's chain method

W1
W2
W3

Each week you hit 3+ workouts = chain link forged. Visual chain grows. Breaking it resets. Powerful psychological pull to not break.

4

Activity Ring (Apple Watch style)

Atomic Habits: "Close your rings" — clear daily/weekly target

1/3
workouts this week
🎁 Close ring → unlock badge

Simple ring fills as you complete workouts. Closing it feels satisfying. Compact and familiar from Apple Watch.

5

Streak Flame + XP Level

Atomic Habits: "Identity-based habits" — become a person who works out

🔥
4-week streak
Level 3 — Dedicated
60/100 XP → Level 4: Warrior

Earn XP per workout. Level up with titles (Beginner → Dedicated → Warrior → Beast → Legend). The flame grows with your streak.

8

Compact Segment Bar

Atomic Habits: "Reduce friction" — minimal, always visible, no clicks needed

1 down, 2 to go 🏆

Ultra-compact. Sits inside the calendar header row. Segments fill, trophy appears when done. Zero friction to see progress.

💡 My Recommendation

Combine #8 (compact bar) as the always-visible inline indicator above the calendar + #3 (chain links) as a longer-term streak motivator below it + #5 (XP/Level) in the Progress tab for identity building.

This gives you: immediate feedback (bar fills), loss aversion (don't break chain), and identity shift (level up titles).
