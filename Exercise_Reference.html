🏋️ Exercise Reference Guide

All exercises with form cues, equipment, and sets — PPL + Arms Split

💪 PUSH DAY — Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
🫁 Chest (Primary)
Push-ups (Warm-up)
2 × 15 reps
🔧 Push-up Board · Bodyweight
Form: Hands shoulder-width, body in plank.
Lower until chest nearly touches floor.
Elbows at 45° — not flared.
Exhale pushing up.
DB Chest Press (Flat)
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Bench flat · 10–12.5 kg
Form: Lie flat, feet on floor.
Press dumbbells straight up from chest.
Squeeze chest at top.
Shoulder blades pinched together throughout.
Lower slowly.
Incline DB Press
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Bench 30-45° · 7.5–10 kg
Form: Bench at 30-45°.
Press straight UP (not towards face).
Targets upper chest.
Don't let elbows drop below shoulder line.
Control the negative.
Dumbbell Fly
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Bench flat · 5–7.5 kg
Form: Arms above chest, slight elbow bend (locked).
Open wide in arc until chest stretch.
Squeeze to bring back.
Think "hugging a tree." Use lighter weight.
🔴 Shoulders (Secondary)
Overhead DB Press
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Bench 75-80° · 7.5–10 kg
Form: Sit upright.
Start at ear level, palms forward.
Press straight up.
Don't arch lower back.
Can do standing too.
Lateral Raises
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Standing · 2.5–5 kg
Form: Slight elbow bend.
Raise arms to sides until parallel (T-shape).
Lead with elbows, not wrists.
Light weight — shoulders fatigue fast.
🔺 Triceps (Finisher)
Tricep Pushdown (Band)
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Pull-up Bar + Resistance Band
Form: Band over bar.
Elbows tight to body.
Push down until arms extended.
Squeeze triceps.
Only forearms move — upper arms locked.
Overhead Tricep Ext (Band)
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Resistance Band (step on it)
Form: Step on band, hold behind head.
Extend arms up, elbows pointing forward near ears.
Full range of motion is key.
🔙 PULL DAY — Back, Traps, Biceps
🔙 Back (Primary)
Bent-Over DB Row
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Standing bent · 10–12.5 kg
Form: Bend 45° at hips, knees bent, back FLAT.
Pull to lower chest.
Squeeze shoulder blades at top.
Don't round back — critical.
Single-Arm DB Row
3 × 10/side
🔧 Bench + 10–12.5 kg
Form: Knee+hand on bench.
Pull dumbbell to hip, elbow past back.
Feel lat squeeze.
Keep torso parallel, don't twist.
💪
Band Lat Pulldown
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Pull-up Bar + Band
Form: Kneel/sit. Pull band to chest, wide grip.
Squeeze lats at bottom.
Think "elbows into back pockets."
Single-arm version is great for isolation.
Face Pulls (Band)
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Band at face height
Form: Pull towards face, elbows high.
Externally rotate (hands beside ears).
Critical for posture — fixes rounded shoulders.
NEVER skip this.
Band Seated Rows
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Band around feet · seated
Form: Sit, legs extended, band around feet.
Pull to lower chest, squeezing shoulder blades.
Back upright, don't lean back too much.
🔼 Traps (Secondary)
Dumbbell Shrugs
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Standing · 10–12.5 kg
Form: Shrug shoulders straight UP to ears.
Hold 1 sec. Lower slowly.
Don't roll shoulders — straight up/down only.
Arms stay straight.
💪 Biceps (Finisher)
Z-Bar Bicep Curls
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Curl Bar + plates
Form: Grip at angled grips.
Curl by bending elbows only — upper arms don't move.
Squeeze at top. No swinging.
Z-bar reduces wrist strain.
Hammer Curls
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Standing · 7.5–10 kg
Form: Palms facing each other (neutral grip).
Hits brachialis (outer arm thickness) + forearms.
No swinging. Can alternate arms.
🦵 LEGS DAY — Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
🦵 Quads & Glutes (Primary)
Barbell Deadlift
3 × 8 reps
🔧 Deadlift Barbell + plates
Form: Feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot.
Back FLAT, chest up.
Drive through heels. Bar close to body.
Slow and controlled — no ego lifting.
Goblet Squats
3 × 12 reps
🔧 1 heavy DB at chest · 10–12.5 kg
Form: Hold DB at chest.
Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
Squat until thighs parallel.
Knees track over toes. Push through heels.
Lunges
3 × 10/leg
🔧 DBs at sides · 5–7.5 kg
Form: Step forward, both knees 90°.
Front knee over ankle — never past toes.
Push back through front heel.
VVIP exercise.
🍑 Glutes & Hamstrings (APT Fix)
Hip Thrust (Bench)
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Bench + barbell/heavy DB
Form: Upper back on bench.
Drive hips UP squeezing glutes.
Full extension (straight line shoulders→knees).
Key APT fix.
Romanian Deadlift (DB)
3 × 10 reps
🔧 7.5–10 kg DBs
Form: Slight knee bend (locked).
Hinge at hips, lower DBs along legs.
Feel hamstring stretch.
Drive hips forward to return.
Critical for APT.
Bulgarian Split Squat
3 × 8/leg
🔧 Bench + 5–7.5 kg DBs
Form: Back foot on bench.
Lower until front thigh parallel.
Stay upright.
Start bodyweight if too hard.
🦶 Calves (Secondary)
Calf Raises (Step)
3 × 20 reps
🔧 Stepping Block + 10 kg DBs
Form: Stand on step edge, heels hanging off.
Rise high on toes, squeeze 1 sec.
Lower below step for stretch.
High reps — calves are stubborn.
🔧 Mobility (APT Fix)
Hip Flexor Stretch
2 × 30s/side
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: Kneel, push hips forward.
Feel deep stretch in front hip of back leg.
Hold 30s.
Do every leg day — loosens tight hip flexors.
Pelvic Tilt Practice
2 × 15 reps
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: Lie on back, knees bent.
Flatten lower back to floor by tilting pelvis up.
Hold 3s.
Mind-muscle APT correction.
🎯 ARMS + ABS DAY — Biceps, Triceps, Core
💪 Biceps (Primary)
Incline DB Curl
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Bench 45° · 5–7.5 kg
Form: Lie on incline, arms hanging.
Curl without moving upper arms.
Incline pre-stretches bicep for more activation.
Slow 3-sec negatives.
Preacher Curl (Bench)
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Bench as arm pad · 5–7.5 kg
Form: Use incline bench as preacher pad.
Arm rests, curl up.
Complete isolation — no cheating. Full range.
Hammer Curls
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Standing · 7.5 kg
Form: Neutral grip (palms facing).
Builds arm thickness.
Try cross-body hammers for extra squeeze.
🔺 Triceps (Secondary)
Tricep Pushdown (Band)
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Pull-up Bar + Band
Form: Band over bar.
Push down, elbows pinned.
Squeeze at full extension.
Bread-and-butter tricep move.
Overhead Tricep Ext (DB)
3 × 10 reps
🔧 Seated · 1×10 kg DB both hands
Form: One DB, both hands behind head.
Extend straight up.
Elbows forward, don't flare.
If wrist pain, use band version.
Diamond Push-ups
2 × 12 reps
🔧 Bodyweight · Gym Mat
Form: Hands together (diamond shape) under chest.
Lower to hands.
Shifts all load to triceps.
From knees if too hard.
🔥 Abs & Core (APT Fix)
Crunches
3 × 20 reps
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: Knees bent, hands behind head (don't pull neck).
Curl upper body — lift shoulder blades off mat.
Feel it in abs, not neck.
Leg Raises
3 × 12 reps
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: Lie flat, hands under hips.
Raise straight legs to 90°, lower slowly without touching floor.
Lower back pressed to mat.
Plank
3 × 30-60s
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: Forearms on mat, body straight.
Squeeze abs+glutes.
Don't sag or pike.
Start 30s, build to 60s.
🐛
Dead Bug
3 × 10/side
🔧 Gym Mat
Form: On back, arms up, knees 90°.
Extend opposite arm+leg.
Keep lower back flat.
Excellent for APT correction.
🦵 Ankle Weight Finishers
🦵
Lying Hamstring Curls
3 × 15 reps
🔧 Mat + 2 kg Ankle Weights
Form: Face down, ankle weights on.
Curl heels to glutes.
Squeeze hamstrings at top.
Great isolation without machines.
🍑
Donkey Kicks
3 × 12/side
🔧 Mat + 2 kg Ankle Weights
Form: All fours, kick leg up+back at 90° knee.
Squeeze glute at top.
Don't arch back.
Targets glutes specifically.
