PUSH = Chest + Shoulders + Triceps — All exercises where you PUSH weight away from your body. ~45-55 min total.

Chest

Primary
1
Push-ups (Warm-up)
Bodyweight • Push-up Board
2×15▶
Warm-up: activate chest & triceps
Equipment
Push-up BoardGym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Hands shoulder-width apart on push-up board grips
  • Body in a straight plank from head to heels — engage core
  • Elbows at 45° (not flared 90°, not tucked 0°)
  • Lower until chest nearly touches floor, push up explosively
  • Exhale pushing up, inhale going down
  • DON'T let hips sag or pike up — core tight
  • DON'T half-rep — full range only
2
DB Chest Press (Flat)
Bench flat • 10–12.5 kg dumbbells
3×12▶
Flat bench dumbbell press — foundation chest exercise
Equipment
Bench (flat, level 4)10–12.5 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Lie flat, feet firmly on floor, natural back arch
  • Pinch shoulder blades together — keep pinched ENTIRE set. #1 chest press rule!
  • Press dumbbells straight up from chest until arms fully extended
  • Lower slowly (3 sec) until dumbbells at chest level, elbows 45°
  • Squeeze chest at top for 1 second
  • DON'T bounce weights off chest
  • DON'T flare elbows to 90° — keeps shoulders safe
  • DON'T lift feet off floor or arch excessively
3
Incline DB Press
Bench 30-45° • 7.5–10 kg
3×10▶
Incline press targets the upper chest
Equipment
Bench (level 3-4, 30-45°)7.5–10 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Set bench to 30-45° — too steep (60°+) shifts to shoulders
  • Press straight UP towards ceiling, NOT towards face
  • Shoulder blades pinched against bench, chest pushed out
  • Lower to upper chest level, feel the stretch
  • DON'T let elbows drop below shoulder line
  • DON'T use momentum — control every inch
4
Dumbbell Fly
Bench flat • 5–7.5 kg (lighter!)
3×12▶
Equipment
Bench (flat)5–7.5 kg dumbbells (lighter!)
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Arms extended above chest with SLIGHT bend in elbows (15-20°)
  • Lock the elbow angle — stays same throughout. NOT a press!
  • Open arms wide arc until chest stretch, don't go too deep
  • Squeeze chest to close — "hugging a tree"
  • Use LIGHTER weight — isolation, not strength. Ego lifting = shoulder injury
  • DON'T go too deep — stop at stretch, not pain

Shoulders

Secondary
5
Overhead DB Press
Bench 75-80° or standing • 7.5–10 kg
3×10▶
Overhead press — king of shoulder exercises
Equipment
Bench (near-vertical, 75-80°)7.5–10 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Start at ear level, palms facing forward
  • Press straight up until arms locked overhead
  • Lower with control back to ear level
  • DON'T arch lower back — use bench back for support
  • Seated (safer) or standing (more core engagement)
6
Lateral Raises
Standing • 2.5–5 kg
3×15▶
Equipment
2.5–5 kg dumbbells (LIGHT is fine!)
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Slight bend in elbows (locked throughout)
  • Raise to sides until parallel (T-shape)
  • Lead with ELBOWS, not wrists — imagine pouring water from jugs
  • Lower slowly (3 sec) — don't drop
  • LIGHT weight is correct — 2.5 kg is fine! Side delts are tiny muscles
  • DON'T swing or use momentum to get them up
  • DON'T shrug shoulders up — keep DOWN and back

Triceps

Finisher
7
Tricep Pushdown (Band)
Pull-up Bar + Resistance Band
3×15▶
Equipment
Pull-up Bar (door)Resistance Band (Medium/Heavy)
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Hook band over pull-up bar, grip both hands shoulder-width
  • Pin elbows TIGHT to body — they don't move AT ALL. Only forearms move.
  • Push down until fully extended, squeeze triceps 1 sec
  • Let band pull back slowly to chest level
  • DON'T let elbows drift forward or flare out
  • DON'T lean over — stand upright, slight forward lean only
8
Overhead Tricep Extension (Band)
Step on band • Arms overhead
3×12▶
Equipment
Resistance Band
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Step on band center, hold ends behind head with both hands
  • Extend arms straight up overhead
  • Elbows point FORWARD near ears — don't let them flare wide
  • Lower behind head with control, feel tricep stretch at bottom
  • Full range — all the way down, all the way up
  • DON'T arch back — keep core tight
PULL = Back + Traps + Rear Delts + Biceps — All exercises where you PULL weight toward your body. ~45-55 min total.

Back

Primary
1
Bent-Over DB Row
Standing bent 45° • 10–12.5 kg
3×10▶
Bent-over row — builds back thickness
Equipment
10–12.5 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Bend at hips 45°, knees slightly bent, back FLAT (neutral spine) — CRITICAL
  • Pull dumbbells to lower chest/hip
  • Squeeze shoulder blades together at top for 1 sec
  • Lower slowly until arms fully extended
  • DON'T ROUND YOUR BACK — the most important rule. If back rounds = lighten weight immediately.
  • DON'T use momentum to swing weights up
2
Single-Arm DB Row
Bench support • 10–12.5 kg
3×10/side▶
Equipment
Flat bench10–12.5 kg dumbbell
✅ Form — Key Points
  • One knee + hand on bench, other foot planted wide for stability
  • Pull dumbbell from full hang to hip — elbow PAST your back
  • Feel the LAT working — think "pull from elbow, not hand"
  • Torso stays parallel to floor, don't twist at the top
  • DON'T rotate torso to cheat the weight up
  • DON'T shrug shoulder — keep it DOWN and back
3
Band Lat Pulldown
Pull-up Bar + Band • Kneel or sit
3×12▶
Equipment
Pull-up Bar (door)Heavy Resistance Band
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Loop band over bar, kneel or sit underneath
  • Pull band down to upper chest with wide grip
  • "Elbows into back pockets" — drive DOWN and BACK with elbows, not hands
  • Lean slightly back (10-15°) for better lat engagement
  • Single-arm version excellent for isolating each lat
  • DON'T pull with biceps — think lat squeeze
4
Face Pulls ⭐
Band at face height • NEVER SKIP THIS
3×15▶
⭐ THE most important exercise for posture. Fixes rounded shoulders from desk work. Do EVERY pull day and optionally push day too.
Equipment
Resistance BandDoor anchor or Pull-up Bar
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Band at face height (hook on bar/door)
  • Pull towards face, elbows HIGH and WIDE (not down)
  • Externally rotate at end — hands beside ears, "hands up" position
  • Squeeze between shoulder blades for 2 seconds
  • Light resistance, high reps — this is posture work, not strength
  • DON'T pull to chin/chest — pull to FACE level
  • DON'T use heavy band — defeats the purpose
5
Band Seated Rows
Band around feet • Seated on floor
3×12▶
Equipment
Heavy Resistance BandGym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Sit on floor, legs extended, band looped around feet
  • Pull band to lower chest/stomach, squeeze shoulder blades together
  • Back upright — don't lean back excessively to cheat
  • Tempo: 2 sec pull, 1 sec hold, 3 sec release
  • DON'T round forward on the release

Biceps

Finisher
7
Z-Bar Curls
Curl Bar + plates • 7.5–12.5 kg total
3×12▶
Strict form > heavy weight for biceps
Equipment
Z-Bar (EZ curl bar)Weight plates
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Grip at angled sections — less wrist strain than straight bar
  • Upper arms DON'T MOVE — pin to sides. Only forearms curl up.
  • Squeeze biceps hard at top for 1 sec
  • Lower with control (3 sec) — the negative = where growth happens
  • DON'T swing body or use back to curl up
  • DON'T half-rep — full extension at bottom
8
Hammer Curls
Standing • 7.5–10 kg
3×10▶
Equipment
7.5–10 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Palms face each other (neutral/hammer grip) throughout
  • Curl keeping neutral grip entire movement
  • Targets brachialis (outer arm thickness) + forearms
  • Can alternate arms or do both simultaneously
  • Try cross-body hammers (curl towards opposite shoulder) for variety
  • DON'T let wrists rotate — hammer grip locked the whole set
LEGS = Quads + Hamstrings + Glutes + Calves + APT Fixes — Compound movements + mobility. ~50-60 min total.

Quads & Glutes

Compound
1
Barbell Deadlift
Deadlift barbell + plates • KING exercise
3×8▶
The deadlift — king of all exercises. Respect it.
⚠️ Perfect form required. No ego lifting. This exercise demands respect.
Equipment
Deadlift BarbellWeight PlatesGym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot (bar touches shins)
  • Back FLAT, chest UP, shoulders directly over bar
  • Drive through heels — hips and shoulders rise together
  • Bar stays close to body (should scrape shins/thighs)
  • Lock out by squeezing glutes at top, NOT by leaning back
  • DON'T ROUND YOUR BACK — #1 injury risk in the gym
  • DON'T jerk off the floor — smooth, controlled pull
  • DON'T look up (hyperextend neck) — neutral neck throughout
2
Goblet Squats
1 heavy dumbbell at chest • 10–12.5 kg
3×12▶
Equipment
10–12.5 kg dumbbell (held vertically)
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Hold dumbbell vertically at chest ("goblet" position)
  • Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out (15-30°)
  • Squat to parallel or below — "sit back" into your heels
  • Knees track over toes — push knees OUT, never let them cave inward
  • Chest up, core tight, push through heels to stand
  • DON'T let knees cave inward (valgus)
  • DON'T round lower back at the bottom
3
Lunges ⭐
DBs at sides • 5–7.5 kg • VVIP exercise
3×10/leg▶
Lunges — VVIP for quads, glutes, and balance
Equipment
5–7.5 kg dumbbells at sides
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Step forward into a lunge, lower back knee towards floor
  • Both knees at 90° at the bottom position
  • Front knee stays over ankle — NEVER drifts past toes
  • Push back through front heel to return to standing
  • Alternate legs, keep torso upright throughout
  • DON'T let front knee shoot past toes
  • DON'T lean forward — upright torso

Glutes & Hamstrings

APT Fix
4
Hip Thrust
Back on bench • Heavy DB/barbell on hips
3×12▶
Key APT Fix — Strengthens weak glutes which is the #1 cause of anterior pelvic tilt. Can significantly improve posture alone.
Equipment
BenchHeavy dumbbell or barbell on hips
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Upper back resting on bench edge, feet flat on floor hip-width apart
  • Drive hips UP by squeezing glutes HARD
  • Full hip extension at top — straight line from shoulders to knees
  • Chin tucked, look forward not at ceiling (prevents neck strain)
  • Hold top position for 2 seconds
  • DON'T hyperextend past straight — lower back risk
  • DON'T push through toes — drive through heels
5
Romanian Deadlift (DB)
7.5–10 kg • Hamstring specific
3×10▶
Hamstring specific — critical for APT. Weak hamstrings allow pelvis to tilt forward.
Equipment
7.5–10 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Stand straight, slight knee bend (knees stay at this angle)
  • HINGE at hips — push butt BACK, lower dumbbells along legs
  • Feel deep hamstring stretch — stop when you feel max stretch
  • Drive hips forward to return to standing, squeeze glutes at top
  • Back stays FLAT entire movement — natural arch maintained
  • DON'T round your back — stop before form breaks
  • DON'T bend knees more during descent — this is a HINGE, not a squat
6
Bulgarian Split Squat
Back foot on bench • 5–7.5 kg or bodyweight
3×8/leg▶
Equipment
Bench5–7.5 kg dumbbells (or bodyweight)
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Back foot on bench (laces down), front foot ~2 feet ahead
  • Lower straight down until front thigh is parallel
  • Torso stays upright
  • Start bodyweight only if balance is hard — this exercise is brutal, no shame in BW
  • Push through front heel to rise
  • DON'T lean forward excessively

Calves

Calf Raises
Stepping Block • 10 kg DBs
3×20▶
Equipment
Stepping Block / Stair edge10 kg dumbbells
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Stand on step edge, heels hanging off into space
  • Rise as HIGH as possible, squeeze calf hard 1 sec at top

Mobility

APT Rehab
8
Hip Flexor Stretch
Kneeling • Do EVERY leg day minimum
2×30s/side▶
Every leg day minimum. Do daily if possible. Tight hip flexors are the #1 cause of APT (anterior pelvic tilt).
Equipment
Gym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Kneel on one knee (lunge position on mat)
  • Push hips forward — feel deep stretch in front of back hip
  • Torso upright — don't lean forward
  • Hold 30 seconds, breathe deeply, switch sides
  • Squeeze glute of back leg — intensifies the stretch
  • For deeper stretch: raise same-side arm overhead
9
Pelvic Tilt (Lying)
Mat • Mind-muscle APT correction
2×15▶
Equipment
Gym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat on floor
  • Flatten your lower back completely against the floor (think: belly button to spine)
  • Hold 3 seconds, release, repeat
  • Mind-muscle connection — teaches your body what correct pelvis position feels like
  • Progress: do while standing against wall

Mat • Upper abs
3×20▶
Equipment
Gym Mat

  • Knees bent, feet flat, hands behind head (light support)
  • Curl upper body up — lift shoulder blades off mat
  • Feel it in ABS not neck — tongue on roof of mouth, look at ceiling

Mat • Lower abs

Equipment
Gym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Lie flat on back, hands under hips for support
  • Raise straight legs to 90°
  • Lower slowly — stop just before touching floor, repeat

9
Plank
Mat • Core stability
3×30-60s▶
Equipment
Gym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Forearms flat on mat, body in perfectly straight line from head to heels
  • Squeeze abs + glutes simultaneously
  • Breathe normally — don't hold breath
  • DON'T let hips sag (most common) or pike up
  • Start 30 seconds, build to 60 — quality over duration
10
Dead Bug ⭐
Mat • Best deep core/APT exercise
3×10/side▶
⭐ Excellent APT correction exercise. Trains deep core stability without hip flexor involvement. If your back arches during this — your core isn't engaging properly.
Equipment
Gym Mat
✅ Form — Key Points
  • Lie on back, arms pointing to ceiling, knees at 90°
  • Extend opposite arm + leg slowly (right arm back, left leg out)
  • Lower back stays COMPLETELY FLAT on floor the entire time
  • Alternate sides slowly — this is NOT a speed exercise
  • DON'T let back arch — if it does, don't extend as far

🎯 10 Golden Form Rules

🧠

Mind-Muscle

Feel the target muscle. If not, weight too heavy or form wrong.

💨

Breathe

Exhale on effort. Inhale on lowering. Never hold breath.

🐢

Control Negative

2-3 seconds going down. That's where growth happens.

⚠️

No Ego Lifting

Can't do 8 clean reps? Weight too heavy. Drop it.

🔊

Full ROM

Full range of motion. Half reps = half results.

🏗️

Neutral Spine

Never round back on rows/deadlifts.

⏱️

Rest 60-90s

60s isolation, 90s compound. Timer, not phone.

📈

Overload

Add reps first (10-12-15), then +2.5 kg weight.

🔥

Warm Up

5 min walk + light set before heavy work.

📊

Track It

Log weight, reps, sets. Can't measure = can't improve.
